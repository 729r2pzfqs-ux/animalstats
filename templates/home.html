About the numbers

Every animal page answers the same set of questions with typical adult figures: lifespan in the wild and in human care, weight, height or length, top speed, gestation or incubation period, diet, habitat and IUCN Red List status. Taxonomy and conservation status are cross-checked against Wikidata, and each page links to its sources.

Animals vary. Males and females often differ, island populations differ from mainland ones, and the most quotable number is not always the best supported. Where sources disagree we use the conservative figure and say so. More about our method.
